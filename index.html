Good to know

Questions before your first session?

The essentials about booking, recordings, AI Review and privacy.

We’re here to helpChat with our team, Mon–Sun, 8:00 AM–9:00 PM.
How long is a café session?+

Each guided speaking session lasts 90 minutes and normally has 2–7 learners.

Do I need to prepare anything?+

You will receive a short Learning Kit with useful phrases and prompts before the session.

What if I cannot find a session that fits me?+

Use the filters to explore nearby levels and dates, or join the waitlist for a new session.

How is my data and recording used?+

Recording starts only after consent and is used to create your private learning evidence and report.

How does the AI Review work?+

AI drafts your report from the session recording and feedback notes. A teacher reviews it before release.

Can I join with friends?+

Yes. Share the session link, but every learner must meet the level requirement and book their own seat.
